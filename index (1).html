Gas safety at home

A few habits keep your family safe.

  • Keep the cylinder upright and in a ventilated place.
  • Turn off the regulator when you are not cooking.
  • Check the rubber tube for cracks every few months.
  • Keep the cylinder away from heat and open flames.
  • Never use a damaged regulator or tube.
  • Check the seal before accepting a new cylinder.
If you smell gas: do not switch any light or appliance on or off. Turn off the regulator, open the doors and windows, move everyone outside, and call us or your gas company's emergency number.
